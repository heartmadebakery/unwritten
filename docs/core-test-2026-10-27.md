# Core test — write-up fidelity and correction capture (Oct 27, 2026)

The simplified core is three things: the lesson video, the correction
clips, the write-up. This test measures the two that software produces —
**how good is the write-up, and how well are corrections captured** — on
text, against planted ground truth. It does not test transcription of real
audio (Whisper on kitchen noise, code-switching, dropped negations): that
can only be measured on the real session.

## Fixture

`samples/lesson_transcript_fixture.md` — a fictional lesson (mother
teaches son her Sunday bread), written as timestamped transcript segments
with **no speaker labels**, because real transcription output has none.
It rambles the way real teaching does: a story mid-step, a shopping
aside, the beginner's self-talk.

**Planted corrections (6)** — the teacher correcting the beginner's craft:
1. `02:08` pressing down with his weight → fold and turn
2. `03:41` kneading by the clock → stop, feel it; done when it stops fighting you
3. `05:38` rolling the loaf like a meatball → tuck under, tight skin
4. `07:04` reaching for more flour → wet your hands instead
5. `08:28` punching the dough down → press until it sighs
6. `10:12` too much oil → just a film (buried mid-anecdote about his grandfather)

**Planted distractors (4)** — must NOT be reported as corrections:
- `00:26` "No, the blue bowl is for rice" — answering which bowl
- `00:48` the teacher correcting her own quantity as she speaks ("a pinch — no, two pinches")
- `06:48` "Don't let me forget, we need eggs" — logistics
- `09:18` the beginner's self-talk ("No wait — I think I've got it")

**Fidelity traps for the write-up:** an oven temperature and bake time
that she never states (she says "hot oven… when it sounds hollow"), the
two-pinch quantity, and three negations that carry technique (don't push
down, don't punch, not more flour).

## Correction capture — results

`pipeline/find_corrections.py` (Muse Spark, `prompts/correction_finder.md`),
one run, transcript only:

| | Found | False alarms | Missed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spark finder | 6 of 6 corrections | 0 of 4 distractors | none |
| Keyword regex baseline | 5 of 6 | all 4 distractors (19 segments flagged) | the buried oil correction |

- Strict segment scoring for the finder: precision 0.83, recall 0.83 —
  the one "miss" is an anchor choice, not a miss: the kneading correction
  is anchored at `03:18` ("Not by the clock") instead of `03:41`
  ("Stop, stop. Feel it"), the same correction beat 23 seconds earlier.
  For clip cutting, that clip would start early.
- The regex baseline scores precision 0.26 and cannot see correction #6
  at all: "that's too much oil, just a film" contains no keyword. This is
  the measured answer to the review challenge that a script could replace
  the model: on text, it can't — not at usable precision.
- Caveat: one run, one fixture, and the fixture's author knew the
  finder's definition. Treat as a smoke result, not a benchmark.

## Write-up — audit

`pipeline/draft_writeup.py` (Muse Spark, stenographer prompt; named `draft_card.py` at run time), one run.
Thirteen steps plus a "What Mother knows" section. Checked line by line
against the transcript:

- **All four fidelity traps avoided.** No oven temperature and no bake
  minutes invented ("Hot oven — knock on the bottom, when it sounds
  hollow, it's done"). Salt is **two** pinches. All three technique
  negations survive: "you don't push down," "don't punch it," "not more
  flour."
- **All stated facts present:** 3 cups flour (white bowl), 1 cup warm
  water "like bath water," small spoon of yeast; rise "about an hour,
  until it doubles" — her numbers, quoted, not supplied.
- **Distinctive phrasing preserved:** "stops fighting you," "just until
  it sighs," "skin gets tight, like a drum."
- **Zero additions detected:** every step and every "What Mother knows"
  line traces to a transcript segment. (The wisdom lines were drawn from
  the lesson itself — there was no separate Q&A in this fixture — and
  none of them put words in her mouth.)
- Steps were split finer than the spoken flow (13 steps for 7 actions);
  acceptable for a write-up someone cooks from.

## What this does and doesn't establish

Established, on clean text: correction capture is accurate and decisively
better than keyword search; the write-up drafter, under the stenographer
rule, resists exactly the inventions it was suspected of (numbers,
temperatures, dropped negations). Not established: anything about real
audio, a real teacher's less tidy speech, or whether the corrections in a
real session are this legible. The real session remains the real test.
